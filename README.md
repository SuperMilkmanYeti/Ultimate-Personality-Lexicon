#Ultimate Personality Lexicon

A curated, deduplicated list of ~815 English personality words with definitions, released under **CC0 1.0** (Public Domain).

Use it for your business or personal projects. It is a tool that can't be found anywhere else, highly useful for writing in Books, Movies, Video Game Development, and Music.

## What's in this repo
The complete dataset is available in the **Releases** section on the right, or directly in the file list as a `.csv`.

## Provenance
Definitions sourced from [Open English WordNet](https://github.com/globalwordnet/english-wordnet) (CC BY 4.0). Roughly 50 definitions were manually authored for words WordNet lacks.
